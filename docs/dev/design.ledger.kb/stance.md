# stance

The posture everything else stands on: what terminates a regress, and what a
distinction has to earn. Prior to everything; cites nothing.

- `prior:` — none
- `ontology:` regress, act, author, authority, judgment, distinction, ontology
- `defeated by:` a different answer to what stops a regress — a foundation that
  is a truth rather than an act

- REGRESS_ACT+: Every regress in this design terminates in an act, not in a further truth. Three of them run here — inference, design, authority — and one pattern stops all three: someone does something, and is named for it
- AUTHOR_ACTS+ <- REGRESS_ACT+: An act has an author. Authority is a property of acts, never of propositions — nothing is authoritative; someone authored something
- NAME_THE_JUDGE+ <- AUTHOR_ACTS+: Where a judgment is recorded, its judge is recorded with it. An unattributed judgment is a hidden oracle, and hidden oracles are the failure this design exists to prevent
- PARSIMONY+: Prefer the smaller ontology. A distinction earns its place by what it lets us state, not by what it lets us tell apart

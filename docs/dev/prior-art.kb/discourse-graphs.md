# Discourse graphs

Joel Chan's discourse-graph practice (Roam and Obsidian research
communities): questions, claims, evidence, and sources as typed nodes
with typed links, authored incrementally during reading and synthesis.
The direct ancestor of `Skill(llm-discourse-graph)` and this repo's own
discourse kbs.

**Gems:**

- The question / claim / evidence typing survives contact with real
  research practice — the type system is small enough to use at reading
  speed.
- Incremental formalization: prose notes become typed nodes only when
  reuse demands it — the gradually-elaborated continuum, practiced
  socially before it was ever mechanized.

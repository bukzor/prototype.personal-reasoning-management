# The home quarry — bukzor's own near-misses

`bukzor.kb/` holds the user's prior systems, each a near-miss of PRMS.
Read together they are one finding, not ten: every organ of this design
was built and lived-in somewhere — substrate (`llm-kb`), typed
references (`llm-discourse-graph`), standing (`llm-claim-ledger`),
norms (`llm-design-kb`), obligations (`llm-subtask`), decisions
(`llm-collab`, `planning-framework`), mandates (`must-read.kb`), even
the kernel theory itself (`ideation.epistemics`, where PRMS's earlier
codename was ACS) — and every one misses the same way: the graph is
walked by eye, not by machine. Agent and operator read, correct, and
improve edges as they browse, and that keeps the corpora alive — but
the only systematic checks are schema validation and path existence
(`llm.kb-validate`, `llm.kb-validate-links`). Standing, staleness, and
support are adjudicated incidentally, wherever attention happens to
land; supersession is social, firing is honor-system. The family's
collective lesson is that conventions accumulate organs but the
checkers stop at well-formedness, which is why PRMS is procurement of
an engine (`PROCURE!`, `CHECKER`) rather than an eleventh convention.

The second family-wide lesson is a gradient in capture cost:
`planning-framework` (taxonomy-first, six tiers before any content)
died of ceremony; `llm-kb` and its descendants (file = item, `ls` =
index) survived years of daily use. `SPAN!`'s informal pole and
`../user-stories.kb/capture-without-ceremony.md` are that gradient,
legislated.

Ancestry without entries: `research.llm-knowledge-collaboration` — an
LLM-conducted stack survey whose outcome was building `llm-kb` rather
than adopting anything it scored; `prototype.chatfs` and
`llm-chat-librarian` — the provenance flank, turning chat exports into
citable sources (the design ledger's own provenance line is a chatfs
path); `llm-vitals` — a consumer of the kb suite, not an ancestor. The
roster is the operator's remembered set plus a sweep of
`bukzor-agent-skills` and `~/repo/github.com/bukzor`; the operator
suspects more ("there's more, i forget") — add them as they resurface.

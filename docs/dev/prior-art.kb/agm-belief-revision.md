# AGM belief revision

The Alchourrón–Gärdenfors–Makinson postulates (1985) for rational
contraction and revision of a belief set: success, inclusion, vacuity,
recovery, minimal change.

**Gems:**

- The postulates are law-shaped: ready-made candidate theorems about any
  revision operation over a ledger.
- The Levi and Harper identities relate revision to contraction — a
  coherence law between verbs, provable once verbs are functions on the
  value.

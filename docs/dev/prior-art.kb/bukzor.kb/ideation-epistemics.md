# ideation.epistemics — ACS, the direct predecessor

`ideation.epistemics`: the realm where PRMS was designed under its
earlier codename, ACS. Reduces the whole family to a two-axiom basis —
TL, total ledger (nothing enters unlabeled) and RN, refinement norm
(every claim sound, open, or retracted) — and frames each system as a
**port** of that one core to a medium: chat (`llm-claim-ledger`), repo
weight (the `.kb/` graphs), mechanized (ACS/FP2). `ladder.md` audits
the rungs cell by cell; `acs-counterfactual.md` re-runs the fleet's
history with ACS first.

**Gems:**

- The basis reduction itself: porting survival as the kernel's
  validation metric — `SPAN!`'s continuum, stated as ports of one core.
- The ladder's dashes: the middle rung (repo weight) is the least
  built and the one that runs every day — the procurement-relevant
  finding that motivates `PROCURE!`.
- Retraction propagation nearly free: retraction as rename, the link
  checker as the propagator — a checker's power is set by naming
  conventions, not machinery budget.
- The monotonic/dynamic seam: checkers monotonic below, ledger dynamic
  above — `CHECKER`'s placement, arrived at independently.
- Twin degenerate modes: RN without TL is sycophancy; TL without RN is
  a perfectly labeled graveyard.

**Miss:** reached the hardest rung before the nearest one. ACS's
deliverables are schematics, not systems; the realm produced a theory
of the ladder while the rung the operator stands on daily stayed
unbuilt — which is why PRMS is procurement of a working engine rather
than a further design pass.

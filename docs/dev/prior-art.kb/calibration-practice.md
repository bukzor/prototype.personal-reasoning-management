# Calibration practice — forecasting and GRADE

Tetlock-style forecasting (Metaculus, prediction markets): likelihoods
attached to claims with explicit resolution criteria and Brier-scored
track records. GRADE in evidence-based medicine: certainty grades on
claims, moved only by named upgrade/downgrade rules.

**Gems:**

- A likelihood is meaningful only with a resolution criterion and a
  scoring rule — a hard demand on any `likelihood:` field before it
  enters the value.
- GRADE moves certainty by rule (risk of bias, inconsistency,
  indirectness …): confidence revision that is law-shaped rather than
  vibes-shaped.
- Track records make the *judge* auditable, not just the claim —
  calibration is authority's report card.

# Prior art — evidence from other quarries

One external system (or tight school) per file: practice elsewhere whose
mechanisms bear on this design. Entries are evidence, never commitments —
commitments live in `../design.ledger.kb/`. Nothing resolves here:
adopting a gem means a ledger claim citing the entry as provenance, and
the matching `[!QUESTION]` dies in `../user-stories.kb/`.

## Format

- What it is: two or three sentences.
- **Gems:** the specific mechanisms worth stealing — not a survey.
- **Informs:** the ledger claim labels and user-story files the gems bear
  on.

## bukzor.kb/ — the home quarry

The user's own prior systems go in `bukzor.kb/`, not here; this
directory stays external-only. Same format plus a **Miss:** section —
what kept the system short of PRMS. `bukzor.md` is the roll-up.

Revise in place: each entry states what the system teaches today, not a
history of it.

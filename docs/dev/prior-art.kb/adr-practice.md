# Architecture Decision Records

Industry practice: decisions as dated records with a status lifecycle
(proposed / accepted / superseded-by), kept adjacent to the artifact
they govern. Universally adopted because it is nearly free.

**Gems:**

- Supersession as pointer, never deletion — last-wins with provenance,
  as social convention.
- The status field *is* the whole lifecycle: the minimal viable standing
  model, and evidence that even one bit of standing pays.
- Context sections record what was weighed — informal reopeners, waiting
  for a mechanism.

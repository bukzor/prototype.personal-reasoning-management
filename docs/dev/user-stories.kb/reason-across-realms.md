# Reason across realms

My corpus is my reasoning about career, health, purchases, people — not
just DTPL metatheory. Claims in those realms have no formal statements,
contested and partial evidence, and degrees of belief. Ledger discipline —
premises, authority, staleness, laws — must apply to them undiminished,
or the system covers only the realm that needed it least.

**Served by:** CORPUS! (the corpus is all realms; DTPL is merely first),
DEPTH_ATTEST (stated depth is first-class), DEGREE! (uncertainty enters
the value as the certainty axis of a verdict's degree — `likelihood: 0.8`
is a source's judgment recorded, and laws can bound it).

> [!QUESTION] are defeat edges in the value?
> Real defeasible reasoning has rebuttal and undercutting, not only
> conjunctive premises. The theory structure has a Defeated-by relation;
> ClaimMeta as sketched has premises and standing but no defeat edges. If
> defeat is not in the value, no law can quantify over it. Settles in
> `ledger` (what defeat is) and `host` (where it lives in the value).
